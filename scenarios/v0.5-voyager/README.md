## Three finite results / 三个有限结果

1. A normal round trip to another star system, returning a resource legally established as absent in the departure system. Unknown or unexplored never means absent.
2. At least one sustained interstellar logistics route, using real mined material and real vessels, with delivery and actual home-system consumption across multiple complete transport cycles.
3. One controllable, recoverable flight failure and recovery of that exact action's checkpoint, with no rollback of progress covered by a newer primary save.

No Dyson-system work, whole-star-map development, arbitrary automatic layout, globally optimal multistar scheduling, or whole-factory reconstruction is added. Preserve all 0.4 factory/expansion/long-window acceptance, published tags, package bytes, hashes and frozen records. [Outstanding Thunderstore website verification](../../operations/formal-release-2026-10-17.md) is tracked separately and does not block unrelated development or authorize another upload.

## Execution order / 执行顺序

First audit legal DSP `0.10.35.29151` signatures, units and call conditions. Reuse existing same-system flight/checkpoint/station and Foundry/Governor capabilities. The first implementation slice covers native-visible star/resource information, route distance and technology, core/fuel/warper consumption, outward/return reserves, landing/withdrawal conditions and all flight stages. One voyage checkpoint binds to its original action; cancellation, timeout and disconnect reconcile that action, successful arrival retires rollback authority, and a newer covering primary forbids rollback. Offline cases include missing fuel/warpers, invisible targets, stale plans, duplicate commits, interruptions and expired/superseded checkpoints. Removing a guard alone is insufficient.

Then choose the closest suitable native-visible target, lock a finite outward/return budget, make the shortest complete normal round trip, verify actual star/planet/landing/energy/material/resource reads, obtain a finite resource amount and return/save normally. Unseen veins are confirmed only on arrival or through legitimate exploration. If the seed cannot prove the required departure-system absence, record that precise constraint; do not invent absence or fly without a finite bound.

After safe recovery protection and reserve budgets are proven, perform one native, controllable failure inside a single safe voyage and recover its exact checkpoint through the applicable recovery gate. Offline tests and ordinary round trips do not close this live gate. Never deliberately consume the return reserve, edit memory/saves, kill unrelated processes or bypass confirmation.

Finally reuse the validated target for one necessary mining/power/station/connection module. Supply vessels and warpers natively. Before observation declare quantities/rates, inventory trends, energy/consumables and a window covering multiple full real transport cycles; no mechanical 0.4 ten-minute or +/-10% template. Prove mined origin, repeated vessel delivery, replenishment, home consumption and continued transport after normal save/recovery. Preloaded stock, one delivery and correct configuration are insufficient.
