Then choose the closest suitable native-visible target, lock a finite outward/return budget, make the shortest complete normal round trip, verify actual star/planet/landing/energy/material/resource reads, obtain a finite resource amount and return/save normally. After safe recovery protection and reserve budgets are proven, perform one native, controllable failure inside a single safe voyage and recover its exact checkpoint through the applicable recovery gate. Offline tests and ordinary round trips do not close this live gate. Never deliberately consume the return reserve, edit memory/saves, kill unrelated processes or bypass confirmation.

Finally reuse the validated target for one necessary mining/power/station/connection module. Supply vessels and warpers natively.
