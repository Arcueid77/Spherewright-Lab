## First-slice proof boundary / 第一切片证据边界

Before any runtime adoption, audit the legally installed current Native assembly, exact signatures/units/call conditions and exploration visibility. The implementation reuses existing flight/checkpoint/station/Foundry/Governor interfaces; deleting the same-system guard alone cannot satisfy this gate. Complete Plugin Release build, relevant regression and exact committed product CI must pass before Lab locks that source and any required cold deployment.

Required offline cases are missing fuel, missing warpers, invisible targets, stale prepared plans, duplicate submission, each flight-stage interruption, checkpoint expiration and a newer primary superseding it. Flight observations cover launch, cruise, entering/exiting warp, approach, braking, landing, replenishment and failure, with material/energy readback and original-action cancellation/timeout/disconnect accounting. Successful arrival retires checkpoint rollback authority.

Unknown mineral locations stay unknown until permitted exploration or arrival. Departure-system absence needs legal exploration evidence. An unsuitable seed is a concrete constraint to report, not permission for unlimited blind travel. Controlled failure must preserve the return reserve and obey the exact recovery confirmation; ordinary round-trip success is not failure-recovery evidence.
