## First-slice proof boundary / 第一切片证据边界

The legal DSP `0.10.35.29151` signatures, units, call conditions and visibility were audited. The implementation reuses the existing flight/checkpoint/station/Foundry/Governor interfaces; removing the same-system guard alone is insufficient. Complete Plugin build, policy/recovery regression, independent review and exact committed product CI passed for 463. Lab adopts that exact source; its own exact CI must succeed before the required cold deployment.

Missing fuel/warpers, invisible targets, stale plans, duplicates, interruptions, checkpoint expiration/overwriting and newer-primary protection remain explicit coverage obligations. Passing contract/policy/recovery fixtures does not prove Unity's live flight stages. Runtime evidence must cover launch, cruise, entering/exiting warp, approach, braking, landing, replenishment/failure, material/energy readback and original-action cancel/timeout/disconnect accounting. Successful landing retires rollback authority; uncertain end ticks must not create fresh travel allowance.

Unknown mineral locations stay unknown until permitted exploration or arrival. Legal observe-2 research is complete, but no departure-system absence evidence exists yet. An unsuitable seed is a concrete constraint to report, not permission for unlimited blind travel. Controlled failure must preserve the return reserve and obey exact recovery confirmation; ordinary round trips and deployment recovery do not close that gate.
