### 适用范围与已完成成果

本复盘覆盖0.5从开发推进到双候选交审的过程，记录可复用经验，不授予新运行权限，不增加审批门，不改历史回执、资源额度、验收条款或候选包。当前状态只见[活动快照](../../scenarios/v0.5-voyager/current-status.md)及源锁；本文件不是第二份运行交接。

[固定完成截点](https://github.com/Arcueid77/Spherewright-Lab/blob/7baf7277b09a8e4f632d8ff5fef151bfb4b12a0c/scenarios/v0.5-voyager/current-status.md)记录：三条原验收闭合；本轮真实矿源完成保存前两轮、恢复后一轮运输，合计300矿物，母星实际消费；最终双包形成并交审。门1/3保留原证据及源码适用范围。不能把这次成功倒推为所有中间尝试都成功。

这条产品分支从10月19日到最终功能源码有74次提交，其中连续多轮涉及恢复、物理接管及保存语义。最终收尾批次累计计费47992/216000游戏tick，其中历史舍弃4773仍计费；这约为13分20秒计费游戏时间，不是整个项目的总运行或墙钟时间。大量延期来自反复准备、修复与重验，不能简单归因于生产观察必须等待很久。

### 适合下一轮直接复用的工作方式

1. **总体计划：** 维护验收目标、整链依赖、实际剩余预算及关键未知，不提前锁死远期每个动作。
2. **当前阶段：** 定义输入、可观察成果、相关调用、失败保全及与下一阶段的衔接。阶段拆分不意味着新增逐阶段审批。
3. **执行与反馈：** 在现行授权及新鲜Native证明内连续执行；新事实只修正受影响的计划，已完成成果继续保留。
4. **回归重点：** 空站点、付料施工、物理闲置、未供电连接、带货物的运行矿源、保存/菜单/恢复及恢复后续运。至少覆盖实际DTO变体和模式条件，不能只靠全真替身。
5. **当前状态：** 只保留一份权威当前摘要，明确源码/实装/实际加载、最新完整进度、原动作、累计账、首错及下一动作；历史证据另行查阅，避免整本加载。
6. **交付：** 准确源码、实装、功能、包结构、载荷一致性、包级安装测试及正式发布分别陈述；按现有要求选择检查，不增加无关运行或审批步骤。

### 应继续保留的做法

- 保留原始失败、accepted/unknown、原幂等键及累计支出；没有通过重放、改票据或退还旧tick把失败包装成成功。
- 隔离玩家背包矿物，结合矿脉减少、带货物、船运及真实母星消费证明来源，恢复后另观察新的循环。
- 实测供电6998而非名义7000J/tick，按真实吞吐证明结果，没有虚增2J或伪称满功率。
- 功能完成后再生成最终同源双包，保留隐私扫描精确误报归类；“无真实/未知泄露”不改写成原扫描零命中。

### Scope and accepted outcome

This retrospective covers Voyager0.5 through owner-review candidates. It adds no execution authority or approval gate and changes no receipt, resource limit, acceptance clause or archive. Use the active snapshot and source lock for current facts; this document is not another operational handoff.

At the fixed completion cut linked above, all three original clauses were closed. The new logistics evidence proves two cycles before Save and one after recovery,300 mined ore and real home consumption. Clauses1/3 retain their historical source scope. Final same-source archives were prepared for review. This outcome does not retrospectively make intermediate failures successful.

The product branch contains74 commits from19 October through the final functional source, including repeated recovery, physical-admission and save-semantics repairs. The closing batch conservatively charges47992/216000 game ticks, including4773 historical discarded ticks: about13m20s of charged game time. This is not total project runtime or wall time. ### Reusable execution method

1. Maintain overall acceptance, dependencies, remaining real budgets and key unknowns; do not freeze every distant action.
2. Define each current stage by inputs, observable outcome, calls, failure preservation and linkage. Stages add no conversation approval gates.
3. Execute continuously within existing authority and fresh Native proof. New facts revise affected dependencies only; retain completed results.
4. Regress empty, paid, idle, connected-unpowered, operating-with-cargo and saved/recovered states, including real DTO variants and mode-specific fields. All-true substitutes are insufficient.
5. Keep one authoritative concise current summary for source/install/runtime, complete progress, original actions, spending, first failure and next action; consult history selectively.
6. State source qualification, installation, gameplay, archive structure, payload parity, package installation and publication separately under existing requirements.

### Practices to retain

Original failures, accepted/unknown outcomes, keys and spending remain preserved without replay or fabricated refunds. Player-carried ore stayed isolated from the mining proof. Resource depletion, belt cargo, full vessel cycles and real consumption established provenance and continuation. Actual power6998 versus nominal7000J/tick stayed visible; no2J capacity was invented. Final archives followed functional review, and the exact privacy false-positive classification was preserved rather than describing a nonzero raw scan as zero findings.

The fixed completion snapshot, review record, manifest and source corrections linked in the Chinese section are the evidence index for both languages. Raw private originals remain protected. The old experience ledger, frozen acceptance and archives are unchanged; this is future-development guidance, not a new historical ledger or game execution entry.
